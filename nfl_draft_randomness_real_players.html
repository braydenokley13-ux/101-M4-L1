What You'll Learn

  • Draft picks are like rolling special dice — there's a range of outcomes, not just one sure thing.
  • Even the best prospects can bust, and late-round picks sometimes become superstars.
  • Randomness comes from many places: injuries, coaching, how well a player fits the team, and plain luck.
  • Run experiments to see this for yourself — simulate the same pick many times!

  • The chart shows how many times each outcome happened.
